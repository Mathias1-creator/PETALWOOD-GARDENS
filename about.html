About Petalwood Gardens

A communication-first landscaping studio rooted in two decades of Bay Area craft.

Our story

Built on integrity, grown by hand

Petalwood Gardens began with a simple conviction: a well-tended landscape should feel effortless to the people who live with it. Founded by Juan Ruiz, the company brings more than twenty years of hands-on horticulture and tree care under a name built for the long term — a studio as comfortable shaping a young tree as it is planning an entire property.

What sets Petalwood apart isn't a single technique — it's a way of working. We lead with communication: walking every site in person, explaining the reasoning behind each recommendation, and keeping you informed from the first visit to the final cleanup. Quality and integrity guide the details most people never notice — the clean pruning cut, the properly amended soil, the edge trimmed by hand.

We also know we're guests on your property. Our crews work tidily, tread lightly, and respect the privacy and routine of your home. When we leave, the only thing you should notice is a landscape that looks — and feels — genuinely cared for.

Insured

Carrying liability insurance, so you're covered for the unexpected the entire time we're on your property.

ISA Certified Arborist

The ISA Certified Arborist credential is awarded by the International Society of Arboriculture to professionals who pass a rigorous exam and commit to ongoing education. For you, it means tree work guided by science — proper pruning that protects long-term health, honest risk assessment before anything is cut, and decisions that weigh safety, structure and the life of the tree. When a chainsaw is involved, certification is the difference between a confident trim and a costly setback.
