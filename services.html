Our Services

Five disciplines, one team. From the health of a single tree to the design of an entire property — here's how we help.

01 — Arboriculture

Arboriculture

Healthy trees are the backbone of a great landscape. Our ISA Certified Arborist assesses, prunes and protects with an eye on the decades ahead — not just the season.

  • Expert tree pruning and structural shaping
  • Tree removal and stump grinding
  • Health and risk assessment by an ISA Certified Arborist
  • Disease and pest diagnosis
  • Cabling, bracing and preservation
02 — Horticulture

Horticulture

The right plant in the right place changes everything. We build resilient, beautiful plantings tuned to Bay Area microclimates and real-world water limits.

  • Plant selection tailored to Bay Area microclimates
  • Soil health analysis and amendment
  • Seasonal pruning and care schedules
  • Integrated pest management
  • Sustainable, low-water plant strategies
03 — Lawn Care

Lawn Care

A lush, even lawn is the result of a routine, not a one-off. We keep yours green, edged and healthy through every season.

  • Mowing, edging and detail trimming
  • Fertilization programs
  • Aeration and overseeding
  • Weed and crabgrass control
  • Seasonal cleanup
04 — Enhancements

Enhancements

Sometimes a landscape just needs the finishing touches — small, well-placed upgrades that make the whole property feel considered.

  • Seasonal color refreshes
  • Mulch installation and renewal
  • Landscape lighting accents
  • Hardscape touch-ups
  • Property-wide visual upgrades
05 — Landscape Design

Landscape Design

Great spaces start on paper. We translate how you want to live outdoors into a clear, buildable plan — and see it through.

  • On-site assessment and concept direction
  • Custom plant palettes
  • Installation planning and project management
  • Irrigation design considerations
  • Long-term care planning
